WHO WE ARE

Creativity backed by 24+ years of experience.

Scorps Designs is a creative, innovative, and graphic design-oriented company with over 24 years of professional experience in the design, production, and execution of classic, contemporary, and historic artistic projects across Nigeria and Africa.

We combine art, culture, architecture, technology, and visual communication to create distinctive designs that communicate identity, history, beauty, and purpose.

WHAT WE DO

From ideas to remarkable environments.

Our expertise covers large-scale painting, mural art, architectural beautification, graphic design, cultural and heritage artworks, and environmental branding.

We create distinctive artistic designs for buildings, flyovers, bridges, walls, vehicles, books, public spaces, markets, hotels, schools, churches, mosques, residential properties, and government facilities.

OUR TEAM

A multidisciplinary team of professionals.

Our highly skilled and multidisciplinary team comprises professional artists, architects, graphic designers, engineers, project managers, and other creative specialists.

Together, we bring extensive experience, technical expertise, creativity, and attention to detail to every project we undertake.
